## Evidence

- `bin/pc-status:157,179` — `BRIEF_TAGS="disk ram temp smart battery docker tailscale"` is the only source of the brief FAIL list (no lid/power/lock/tmux/timers).
- `bin/pc-status:24` — `ok_if()` defined, never called (`grep -n ok_if` → one hit).

## Survey
