# motion-video reference: measured pitfalls

## Pitfalls (measured 2026-10-19, T439)
- Remotion: first render downloads Chrome Headless Shell (~1 min once); 600 frames 1080p60 = 53 s wall (11.3 fps) first run, 38.8 s (15.5 fps) after; 4K60 via `--scale=2` = 3.4 fps (10 s ≈ 3 min).
- Motion Canvas has no headless CLI: `render.mjs` clicks `#render`, waits for `[data-rendering]` to go away. Spawn Vite as its own process group and kill `-pgid`; killing `npx` left Vite alive holding the pipe, and the caller hung 10 min. With `"type": "module"` in package.json `vite.config.ts` fails (`motionCanvas is not a function`) - keep the template CJS-default. 356 frames 1080p60 in 19.5 s (18 fps); its ffmpeg exporter is low bitrate (190 kbps) - re-encode or switch to the image-sequence exporter for masters.
- Blender EEVEE on Iris Xe: raytracing on + 32 samples = 12 s/frame, off + 16 samples = 4-6 s/frame at 1080p (`MV_RAYTRACE=1`, `MV_SAMPLES`, `MV_SHUTTER` motion blur, default 0.5 = 180°, 0 = off, off in `--draft`); first frame +15 s shader compile; the 5 s 1080p30 shot took 923 s (6.2 s/frame) under load avg ~10, 303 MB of PNGs; 16 samples leave floor noise on glossy planes - 32+ for finals. `gpu` module is unavailable in `-b`, check the driver via `/proc/<pid>/maps` (libgallium + libdrm_intel = iris).
- QSV (`h264_qsv`) fails with "MFX session -9" (no libmfx-gen1.2); VA-API works. Iris Xe has no AV1 hardware encode (decode only) - use SVT-AV1.
- SVT-AV1 prints a banner on stderr; `mv-encode` sets `SVT_LOG=1`.
- Don't render inside the CI window (00:50-01:50): `ci-window && mv-render ...`.
- Drops: mp4 only; delete PNG frames and `mv-render clean` the project.
- Remotion concurrency (T453, 16 threads): flat 2D comp 600 fr 1080p60 = 22.6-24.6 s at 4/6/8/12/16 (flat), 42.8 s at 2; 3D comp with motion blur 300 fr = 28.1 s at 4, 29.4 s at 8, 77.6 s at 16 (GPU contention). Template default `setConcurrency(6)`; never 16 for WebGL.
- `CameraMotionBlur samples={8}` cost 1.8x (49.4 s vs 27.2 s for 300 frames), not 8x.
- `mv-encode --subs` plus `-shortest` cut the video at the last caption (565 of 600 frames); file inputs now use `-t <input duration>`.
- Kenney zips unpack read-only directories; `mv-assets` chmods before moving. FreePD (CC0 music) closed in 2026; music comes from OpenGameArt CC0 pages.
- Kokoro VO for 2 sentences took 122 s wall under load ~20 (model load included).
- T478: reel 3000 frames 1080p60 (3D 11 s with 6-sample blur) rendered in 303.8 s (9.9 fps) at load ~12; LUT + burn encode 132 s. `mv-review` black check is `pix_th=0.03` so dark-ink brands do not read as black; kinetic-type masks need `translateY(135%)`, not 110%, or the word peeks out.
- T783: the T694 hero film shipped its 2D title over the robot 0-7 s and an out-of-frame 3D title in shot 1, seen only after a 7,304.8 CPU s build. Run `mv-layout out/ [--aspect 9x16] [--window T0-T1]` after the plates dump cam*.json (or from a `layout.json` plan) before titles and composite: it projects the subject box through every camera frame (pure Python, ~0.1 s) and FAILs title-over-subject, unseen 3D titles and squashed subject scale; the lens/aspect line (printed for a .glb with no judged keys) covers the frames where the subject is on screen, or `--window T0-T1`; it says `cause` only at the FAIL ratio 1.6 and `note` above 15 % (T1002: the T694 crane's x/y 1.31 at 13.50 s is a note). Rotation keys on the root chain (scene root to the top skin joint and its child joints) are composed into world frames: sigma max / sigma min of world x rest^-1 > 1.15 FAILs `squash <t0>-<t1> s: <joint> shear <r>` (a root turning 90 deg under a [1, 0.6, 1] armature: 2.78); RobotExpressive Dance keys Body (81 keys over the 3 hero clips), shear 1.00. Leaf-joint translation keys (RobotExpressive Walking/Running Foot and PoleTarget IK targets) FAIL squash: T694's robot squashed at 5-6 s played Walking.
