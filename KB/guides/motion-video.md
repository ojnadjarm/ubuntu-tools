## Pitfalls (measured)
- `MV_MODEL_ANIM` (T677): the glTF importer leaves the first clip active plus one NLA track per clip, so a skinned hero played it before; shot.py now clears them (rest pose) unless MV_MODEL_ANIM names a clip. Imported clips are already at MV_FPS (1 s clip = 12 frames at 12 fps). Rest pose != clip frame 1 (fixture: 0.5 % pixels differ), so compare animated vs static at the peak, not frame 0. Fox Run 960x540 4 s 24 fps with blur: 195 s, 0.49 fps.
- `mv-beats` (T578): essentia 2.1b6 RhythmExtractor2013 degara in `~/.local/opt/mv-beats` (wheel sha256 in `INSTALL.sha256`; aubio has no wheel, librosa 1.0 read 117.5 for 120 BPM and took 32 s on first numba JIT). Raw degara beats sit 4-23 ms early (frame grid); mv-beats fits a line when steady and shifts the phase onto a 1 ms energy flux: worst error 1.2 ms (120) / 0.3 ms (97), tempo 119.996 / 97.0. `essentia.standard` wraps ~300 algorithms on import (1.6-2.4 s); mv-beats wraps only 4 (60 s track 1.3 s). Downbeats are a low-end heuristic: check by ear.
- `mv-mix --fit bars` (T589): loops the bed on the longest multiple of 4 bars from the first downbeat >= 40 ms (mv-beats `--downbeats`), joined by a 40 ms qsin crossfade that ENDS on the downbeat (centred on it, the two identical hits sum +3 dB); ends on the last downbeat with 1.5 s left, then a 1.5 s release and silence. 97 BPM fixture: seams 1.0-1.8 ms from the downbeat, largest seam step -46 dBFS, groove error <= 5 ms vs 619 ms with `--fit none` (byte-identical to the old default). Bed under 4 bars -> rc 1. Test `tests/mv-mix-fit.test.sh`.
- `mv-mix --duck-on vo,sfx,game` (T670, default `vo` = byte-identical old output): sidechain key = sum of the named stems (`--game FILE[@SEC[@DB]]` adds game audio, default 0 dB); attack = fastest, release = longest of those classes in `loudness.json` `"duck"` (vo 30/450, sfx 5/250, game 10/350 ms; the key is not a `--target`). Synthetic 220 Hz bed: -19.9 dB under a 4 kHz SFX hit, -21.4 dB under a game burst, back to 0.0 dB 0.45 s after the hit. T686: `duck.<class>.depth_db` (sfx 8, game 6; vo none = uncapped, byte-identical) sets the compressor `mix` = 1 - 10^(-depth/20), so the dip is capped: synthetic bed -6.8 dB under the SFX hit, -5.3 dB under the game burst; T678 trailer bed median 15.8 -> 4.7 dB, max 23.0 -> 5.4 dB (`~/agent-work/evidence/T686/duck.txt`). Test `tests/mv-mix-duck.test.sh`.
- `mv-lut match` (T573) is a global Oklab mean + 3x3 covariance transport (MKL), no pixel correspondence: testsrc2 tinted by colorbalance+eq goes mean dE2000 9.6 -> 1.4 (png) and 10.0 -> 1.4 (mp4, frames at 25/50/75%); identical inputs give a cube 0.0005/255 off identity. It matches overall colour, not per-object; clipped highlights in SRC stay clipped.
- Motion Canvas has no headless CLI; `render.mjs` clicks the editor's `#render`. Killing `npx vite` left Vite holding the pipe and the caller hung 10 min; spawn Vite as a process group and kill `-pgid`.
- `"type": "module"` breaks the Motion Canvas `vite.config.ts` (`motionCanvas is not a function`).
- Legal levels (T565): `mv-encode` clamps Y 16-235 / C 16-240 (10-bit 64-940/960) with `limiter` in a planar format (limiter on nv12 makes h264_vaapi fail "encode issue 24"); lossy encoders still overshoot ~1-10 codes at hard edges, so `mv-review` counts illegal Y on a 2x2 area mask (>= 3 of 4 px) of the 480 px proxy, FAIL > 0.1 % of a frame; the VA-API scaler rings (legal 24-216 testsrc2 reads 0-239), hence ymin/ymax from a 3x3 median at 1/s; `signalstats` costs ~15 CPU-s on a 50 s 1080p60 reel and `erosion` ~12 (both rejected); `fps`/`select` before `hwdownload` of decoder surfaces fails "Failed to sync surface". Review wall +0.3 s (+0.4 s with `--scopes`).
- QSV fails ("MFX session -9"); VA-API h264/hevc works at 75/54 fps. Iris Xe has no AV1 hardware encode.
- Blender EEVEE headless runs on the Iris Xe; 4-6 s/frame at 1080p with 16 samples and raytracing off; 12 s/frame with raytracing on and 32 samples.
